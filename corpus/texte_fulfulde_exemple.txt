Pulaar ko ɗemngal mawngal e nder Afrik hirnaange. Fulɓe ko leñol yaajngol taariingol leyɗeele keewɗe.
Jam tan e kelliinde e cellal ! Noy mbadaa ? Kori jam e kisal e balɗe ?
En ngarii ngam huutoraade karallaagal kesal e nder windude e janngude ɗemngal Fulfulde.
Clavier Fulfulde Latin naftori alkule teeŋtuɗe no ɓ, ɗ, ŋ, ƴ e hamza ’.
Mido yiɗi janngude deftere ndeerewol e gandal mawngal.
Aɗa anndi wonde gandal ko doole mawɗe ngam ɓamtaare leydi e ɓiɓɓe leydi.
Sukaaɓe rewɓe e worɓe fof poti naatde e duɗal ngam heɓde humpito e gannde kesi.
Mawɓe men ina kaala ko fayti e taariika, aadaaji, e pine men belɗe.
Nagge nge ko jawdi mawndi wonande fulɓe e gaynaako kala.
Kosam biraaɗam e kaadam ina weltina ɓerɗe sehilaaɓe e kodo arii e wuro.
So tawii aɗa winnda e ordinateer maa e telefoŋ, autocompletion Fulfulde ina walla maa no feewi.
Woto yejjit jaaraama, belɗo, njarama, a barkiɗi, jooɗo e jam !
